The Analogy

🔑 House Keys Passwords
Just like you don't give keys to strangers, don't share passwords. If you lose them, intruders get in.
🚪 Doors & Windows Operating System
You lock doors at night. Similarly, you must "patch" (update) your OS to seal cracks thieves could slip through.
🚧 Fence/Gate Firewall
A fence stops people from walking onto your lawn. A firewall stops random internet traffic from entering your device.
💎 Jewelry Box Your Data
Photos, documents, bank details. This is what the thieves are actually looking for.
