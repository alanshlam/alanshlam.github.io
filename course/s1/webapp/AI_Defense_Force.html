Defense Strategy

  • Verify Context: Even if the details are right, call the sender on a known number.
  • Check the Domain: Look closely at the sender address (e.g., compny.com vs company.com).
  • Use AI Filters: Tools like StrongestLayer can analyze behavioral patterns to block these.

Detection Checklist

Use these cues to spot AI video/audio:

Video Glitches
  • Unnatural blinking patterns (too fast/slow).
  • Lip sync issues (audio doesn't match mouth).
  • Blurriness around the ears or hairline.
  • Inconsistent lighting on the face vs. background.
Audio/Process
  • Robotic cadence or lack of emotion.
  • Background noise cuts out abruptly.
  • Pro Tip: Establish a "Secret Code" with family/execs for emergencies.
