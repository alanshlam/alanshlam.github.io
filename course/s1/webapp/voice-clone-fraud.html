🗣️ The Mimic

Scammers used AI to clone the voice of the German Parent Company CEO. It copied his accent, tone, and mannerisms perfectly.

⚡ The Target

The Managing Director of the UK subsidiary received the call. Recognizing his "Boss's" voice, he obeyed the instructions immediately.

How Voice Cloning Works

1. Training Data

AI needs to "hear" the target first. Scammers likely downloaded earnings call recordings or YouTube interviews of the German CEO.

2. Synthesis (TTS)

Text-to-Speech engines take the training data and create a model. The scammer types text, and the AI speaks it in the CEO's voice.

3. Real-Time Conversion

Modern tools allow "Speech-to-Speech." The scammer speaks into a mic, and the computer changes their voice to sound like the CEO instantly.

Consumer Defense: When Your Ears Lie

This tech is now used to scam grandparents and families. Here is how to verify a voice.

📞

1. The Hang Up Rule

If you get a distressed call from a loved one asking for money, Hang Up immediately. Call them back on their known mobile number. Scammers cannot answer their real phone.

🔐

2. Family Safe Word

Pick a random word (e.g., "Purple Elephant"). If a family member calls in an emergency, ask for the word. AI clones don't know the password.

⏳

3. Question Urgency

AI scams rely on panic. "I'm in jail," "I'm in the hospital." Pause. Breathe. Ask a question only they would know (e.g., "What did we get Dad for his birthday?").

🔇

4. Limit Public Audio

Be careful with public TikToks or Instagram stories featuring your voice. Scammers can scrape this audio to clone you and scam your parents.
