Why Encrypt?

Encryption turns your data into unreadable code. If you lose your laptop or USB drive, thieves cannot access your files (like HKID copies or bank info) without the key.

💻 Full Disk Encryption (FDE)

Protects the entire hard drive. If the computer is off, data is safe. (e.g., BitLocker, FileVault).

☁️ Secure Cloud Storage

Ensures files are encrypted before leaving your device ("Zero-Knowledge"). Google/Dropbox can't see them.

📱 Mobile Protection

Modern phones are encrypted by default, BUT only if you set a strong Passcode (6+ digits) or Biometric.

🇭🇰 Hong Kong PDPO Tip:
Under Data Protection Principle 4 (Security), data users must protect personal data from unauthorized access. Encrypting USB drives and laptops is the most effective way to comply.

Mobile Encryption (Android & iOS)

Smartphones carry our most sensitive data. Most modern phones are encrypted by default, but you must enable the key.

OS Feature How to Enable / Verify Cost
iOS (iPhone) Data Protection (AES-256) Go to Settings > Face ID & Passcode. Scroll down to see "Data protection is enabled". Set a 6-digit PIN minimum. Free
Android File-Based Encryption Go to Settings > Security > Encryption & Credentials. It should say "Encrypted". Use Biometrics for ease. Free
MDM (SME) Remote Enforcement Use tools like ManageEngine or Samsung Knox to force encryption on all employee phones. ~$10/mo
✅ Quick Action: Set your "Auto-Lock" screen timeout to 30 seconds. Encryption only works when the phone is locked!
