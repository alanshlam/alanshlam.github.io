Common Tips for Individuals & SMEs

Before installing tools, master these behaviors. They stop 80% of threats.

🔄

Update Everything

Enable auto-updates for browsers (Chrome/Edge) and OS. Patches fix 91% of holes.

🔍

Verify URLs

Hover over links before clicking. Look for https://. Use Scameter for weird links.

🚫

Block Ads

Ads can carry malware ("Malvertising"). Use a blocker to stop pop-ups and tracking.

🔑

Don't Save Passwords

Browsers are easy to hack. Use a dedicated Password Manager (e.g., Bitwarden) instead.

🌐

Public Wi-Fi Risk

Avoid banking on coffee shop Wi-Fi. If you must, use a VPN to encrypt your data.

👮

Least Privilege

Deny site requests for Location/Camera unless 100% necessary.
