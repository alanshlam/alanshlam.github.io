Understanding Backups: Simple, Effective, & Ransomware-Proof

The Golden Rule: 3-2-1

Don't put all your eggs in one basket. To guarantee your data survives modern threats, follow this formula:

📂

3 Copies

Total copies of your data. (1 Original + 2 Backups)

💿☁️

2 Types

Different media types. (e.g., Hard Drive + Cloud)

🏠

1 Offsite

Far away from your house/office. (e.g., The Cloud)

⚠️ The New Threat: Ransomware

Today, the 3-2-1 rule isn't enough because hackers can lock your computer AND your attached backup drive.

We need two extra numbers:

  • 1 Immutable/Offline Copy: A backup that cannot be changed (Read-Only) or is physically unplugged (Air-Gapped).
  • 0 Errors: You must TEST your backups to ensure there are zero errors.
