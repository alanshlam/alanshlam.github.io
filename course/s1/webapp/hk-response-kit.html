STOP! Read this first (First 24 Hours)

If you suspect you've been hacked, follow these universal steps immediately.

1
Isolate but Don't Power Off
Disconnect the internet (Wi-Fi/Cable). Do not turn off the PC (you might lose evidence).
2
Secure Credentials
Use a different device to change passwords immediately. Enable 2FA.
3
Call for Help
Contact HKCERT (8105 6060) for confidential advice.
4
Scan & Backup
Run an antivirus scan. Backup clean files only. Do not backup infected files.
