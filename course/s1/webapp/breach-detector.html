Is Your System Compromised?

HKCERT identifies these as the top "Red Flags." If you see these, don't ignore them.

🐌

Performance Drop

Is your computer suddenly slow? Is the fan spinning loud when you aren't doing anything? This could be Ransomware encrypting files in the background.

💳

Account Anomalies

Did you get a "Password Reset" email you didn't ask for? Or a strange small transaction on your bank statement? Hackers test small before stealing big.

🌐

Network Oddities

Is your internet slow? Are there devices on your Wi-Fi you don't recognize? This could be a DDoS attack or someone stealing your bandwidth.

🚨

Antivirus Alerts

Did your antivirus disable itself? Or did it pop up a warning you clicked "Close" on? Never ignore security notifications.

📧

Unknown Files/Emails

Are your contacts complaining about spam coming from you? Or do you see new icons on your desktop you didn't install?

📝

Log Indicators

For advanced users: Do your system logs show failed login attempts at 3:00 AM? This is a "Brute Force" attack.
